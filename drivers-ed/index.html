Driver's Ed · Bucks County

Practice in a white Acura MDX on the roads between Doylestown, New Hope, Quakertown and Warrington: town streets, back roads and the 611 Bypass. Your coach checks your seatbelt, turn signals, stops, lane lines, speed and parking, then gives you a report card.

Controls

  • Before you drive: B seatbelt · hold the brake ↓ and press D for Drive (R reverse, N neutral, P park)
  • Drive: ↑ gas · ↓ brake · ← → steer · H horn · C camera (chase, driver's seat, top-down for parking)
  • Turn signals: Q left · E right. They cancel themselves after the turn.
  • Touch: drag the steering wheel left and right, hold GAS and BRAKE, and tap the gear, signal and seatbelt buttons on the dashboard.
  • Rules: come to a complete stop at stop signs and red lights · signal every turn, lane change, merge and exit · stay right of the double yellow line · keep within 5 mph of the speed limit.
  • Follow the blue marker and the arrow at the top. Tap the mini-map to zoom out.
